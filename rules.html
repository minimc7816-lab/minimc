SERVER RULES
  • 1. Respect all members and staff.
  • 2. No harassment, bullying, or hate speech.
  • 3. Do not spam messages, emojis, or reactions.
  • 4. No NSFW or inappropriate content.
  • 5. Use channels for their correct purpose.
  • 6. No excessive CAPS LOCK.
  • 7. Avoid toxic behavior and unnecessary drama.
  • 8. Do not advertise without permission.
  • 9. No self-promotion in general chats.
  • 10. Respect everyone’s opinions.
  • 11. Do not impersonate staff or members.
  • 12. No sharing personal information.
  • 13. Keep conversations friendly and safe.
  • 14. No offensive usernames or profile pictures.
  • 15. Do not bypass punishments using alt accounts.
  • 16. Listen to moderators and admins.
  • 17. No racism, sexism, or discrimination.
  • 18. Do not flood voice channels with noise.
  • 19. Use appropriate language.
  • 20. No trolling or baiting members.
  • 21. Do not send malicious links or files.
  • 22. No scamming or phishing attempts.
  • 23. Respect privacy in DMs and chats.
  • 24. Do not beg for roles or permissions.
  • 25. Keep memes in meme channels only.
  • 26. No political or religious fights.
  • 27. Avoid repeated arguments in chat.
  • 28. Do not abuse bots or commands.
  • 29. No fake giveaways or fake announcements.
  • 30. English only unless allowed in specific channels.
  • 31. Stay active and positive.
  • 32. No disturbing or shocking content.
  • 33. Use spoilers when necessary.
  • 34. Do not leak private server information.
  • 35. Avoid mic spamming in VC.
  • 36. No inappropriate nicknames.
  • 37. Respect server events and activities.
  • 38. Do not ping everyone without permission.
  • 39. No unnecessary tagging of staff.
  • 40. Keep profile bios appropriate.
  • 41. No cheating discussions if gaming-related.
  • 42. Report rule breakers to staff.
  • 43. Do not start unnecessary conflicts.
  • 44. Respect all age groups.
  • 45. Avoid off-topic discussions in important channels.
  • 46. No threatening or violent behavior.
  • 47. Follow Discord Terms of Service.
  • 48. Staff decisions are final.
  • 49. Breaking rules may result in warnings, mute, kick, or ban.
  • 50. Most importantly, have fun and enjoy the server!
